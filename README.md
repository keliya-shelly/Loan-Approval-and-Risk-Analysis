# 🏦 Loan Approval & Credit Risk Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics & Credit Risk Analysis project** analyzing **30,000 loan applications** to understand loan approval/rejection patterns, identify high-risk customer segments, and uncover the financial and customer factors associated with lending risk.

The project combines **SQL, Python/Pandas, and Power BI** to perform data analysis, derive business insights, and build an interactive dashboard for data-driven lending decisions.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Banks need to evaluate loan applications efficiently while minimizing the risk of approving customers who may default.

The goal of this project is to analyze **30,000 loan applications** and understand:

* Why loans are approved or rejected
* Which customers have higher credit and default risk
* How income, credit score, debt, loan amount, and repayment history influence risk
* Which customer segments require greater attention during lending decisions

The analysis transforms raw loan application data into actionable insights that can support **credit assessment, risk management, and lending decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze loan applications and identify **approval and rejection patterns**
* Identify **high-risk customer segments**
* Understand the relationship between **income, credit score, debt, and loan amount** and credit risk
* Analyze the impact of **repayment history and customer profile**
* Compare loan performance across **regions, loan purposes, and application channels**
* Build an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to support data-driven lending decisions

## 💡 Key Business Questions

This project addresses questions such as:

1. What percentage of loan applications are approved vs. rejected?
2. Which credit-score segments have higher approval rates?
3. How does income affect loan approval and risk?
4. Does higher debt indicate greater lending risk?
5. How does loan amount relate to approval outcomes?
6. Which customer segments appear to be higher risk?
7. How do approval patterns vary across regions?
8. Which loan purposes have different approval/risk patterns?
9. How do application channels compare?
10. What customer characteristics should lenders consider when evaluating risk?

## 📈 Business Value

The analysis can help a lending organization:

* Identify potentially **high-risk customer segments**
* Understand major factors associated with loan approval
* Improve **credit-risk assessment**
* Support more consistent lending decisions
* Monitor approval patterns across business segments
* Make more **data-driven lending and risk-management decisions**

> **Note:** This analysis identifies patterns and relationships in historical loan application data. It is intended as a decision-support analysis and not as a standalone credit approval system.
